## 4. Methodology

- Vacation day = vacation hours / 9 h. Full days are 9 h and half days 4.5 h, so a half day counts as 0.5.
- Working days are Monday to Friday. Weekends are never counted.
- Public holiday exclusion: off. Every Monday to Friday is a working day.
- Employees (history): members with at least one timesheet row in the month. Employees (forecast): members the model forecasts, taken from those with a timesheet row in the latest complete month (lookback = 1 month(s)). The same headcount is held for every forecast month.
- Vacation % = vacation days / available person-days x 100. Availability % = 100 - vacation %. Period figures are sums of days over sums of person-days, not averages of monthly percentages.
- Average vacation days per employee = vacation days / employees.
- Partial months excluded from history: Sep 2022, Sep 2025.

## 6. Technical Details

The recursive feedback issue has been fixed. Predicted vacation months were earlier counted as vacation events in the history features, so the forecast fed on its own output. The event-count features now use observed vacation only, and predicted months cannot add events. The pre-fix diagnosis is in recursive_forecast_diagnostic.md.

## 7. Limitations

- Vacation days assume 9 h per full day. A different day length changes every day count.
- Forecast employees are members active in the lookback window. This can differ from the current headcount, which changes the percentages.
- The holdout is 6 months. Differences between models are indicative only.
- Seasonal naive uses the same month last year and treats missing history as 0. It is kept as the baseline.
- Event-count inputs use observed data only. Other inputs still use predicted hours for forecast months: lags, 3- and 12-month sums, vacation occurrences over 12 months, and historical average and maximum.
- Explanations are XGBoost attributions for the fitted model. They are not causes.
- Holiday counts come from the holiday file. Coverage gaps there affect the holiday input.
- The forecast starts after the last complete month in the data.
